Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Browser storage (local storage) works the same way. This page lists every cookie and storage item nexyrastudio.com uses, and how to control them.

Essential (always on)

These keep the site working and secure. They do not track you, so they do not need your consent.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
themeNexyra Studio (local storage)Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode.Until you clear it
cookie-consentNexyra Studio (local storage)Remembers your cookie choice so this banner doesn't keep appearing.Until you clear it
nx-thanksNexyra Studio (session storage)Passes your name and booking time to the thank-you page after you send a form.Until you close the tab
Turnstile cookies (for example cf_clearance)CloudflareChecks that a form is being sent by a person, not a bot.Up to 1 year, often shorter

Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click Accept, we load Google Analytics, which sets these cookies. If you click Reject, they are never set.

NameProviderPurposeDuration
_gaGoogle AnalyticsDistinguishes one visitor from another.2 years
_ga_<ID>Google AnalyticsKeeps track of your current visit.2 years

We don't use

Advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or cookies that sell your data.

Changing your choice

Click Cookie Settings at any time to accept or reject analytics cookies. Rejecting removes the Google Analytics cookies from your browser. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. Blocking essential storage may stop parts of the site, such as the spam check on our forms, from working.

More information

Read our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information, or email support@nexyrastudio.com with any question.

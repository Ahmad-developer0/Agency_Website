About Us

We Are Digital Architects of Growth

Nexyra Studio is a digital agency in Faisalabad, Pakistan, founded in 2022. We help businesses of every size get found online, win more customers and automate the work that slows them down. We work with clients across Pakistan and, remotely, in the US, UK, Europe and the Gulf.

We keep it simple: a website that loads fast and works on every phone, local SEO so you show up on Google Maps, and marketing you can measure. You work directly with the people doing the work.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our services, pricing and process. Can't find yours? Ask us directly.

What services does Nexyra Studio offer?
We offer online appointment systems, customer CRM, automated reminders, calling systems, business automation, custom software, integrations, web development, app development, and digital marketing & SEO. Open any card in Our Services to see what each one includes.
How much does a project cost?
Every project is priced on its scope, so there is no fixed price list. Book a free call or message us on WhatsApp and we will send you a custom proposal.
Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. The discovery call is a free 30-minute video call on Google Meet or Zoom, with no commitment. After the call we share a custom proposal.
How quickly will you reply?
We reply to every booking and message within 24 hours, by email or WhatsApp.
Where are you based, and do you work with clients abroad?
We are based in Faisalabad, Pakistan, and work remotely with clients in Pakistan and abroad, including the US, UK, Europe and the Gulf. We communicate in English and our working hours overlap with your business day. When you book a call you can pick your own timezone.
Do you build mobile apps?
Yes, we build Android applications in Kotlin and Java, from planning and UI/UX design through API integration, testing and optimization.
What does your process look like?
Four steps: Discovery & Audit, Strategy Blueprinting, Creative Execution, then Optimize & Scale, with transparent reporting throughout.
Can I verify a Nexyra Studio internship certificate?
Yes. Enter the certificate number on our certificate verification page.

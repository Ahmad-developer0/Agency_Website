Booking & CRM

Booking and CRM Systems That Fill Your Calendar

Two problems quietly cost service businesses more than anything else: inquiries that arrive after hours and never get answered, and customers who book and then don't show up. Both are fixable with software, and neither requires you to change how you work.

Online booking

A booking page lets customers choose a real available slot themselves, at eleven at night if that is when they are free. No phone tag, no double bookings, no one writing appointments in a notebook.

  • Live availability — pulled from your actual calendar, with buffer times and breaks respected.
  • Service and staff selection — different durations and prices per service, and per staff member where that matters.
  • Deposits and prepayment — the single most effective way to cut no-shows. Collected through Stripe or Square at booking.
  • Multiple locations — one booking page, separate calendars.
  • Your branding — part of your own site, not a third-party page that looks nothing like your business.

Automated reminders

No-shows are rarely deliberate; people simply forget. An automatic reminder the day before, and again a couple of hours ahead, removes most of them. We set these up over SMS and email — the channels US customers actually read — with a one-tap way to confirm or reschedule, because a customer who reschedules is worth far more than one who silently disappears.

Customer CRM

Once bookings run through a system, you have a customer history worth something. Every appointment, inquiry, note, and payment sits against the right person instead of being scattered across text threads, a notebook, and three phones.

  • Full history for each customer, visible to whoever is on the desk.
  • Segments — who has not been in for six months, who books every month.
  • Follow-up reminders for your team, so repeat business is chased deliberately rather than remembered by luck.
  • Reports: busiest hours, revenue per service, which staff member is booked out.

Calling and follow-up systems

For businesses where the phone is still the main channel, we set up call handling and follow-up so inquiries don't fall through the cracks: missed calls logged as tasks, call outcomes recorded against the customer, and automatic follow-up for quotes that were sent but never answered.

Business automation

Once the data is in one place, the repetitive work can run itself — invoices generated after an appointment, review requests sent two days later, new inquiries pushed to your team channel, daily schedules emailed to staff each morning, stock or capacity warnings. Every hour your team spends retyping information between apps is an hour nobody is paying you for.

Off-the-shelf or custom?

If an existing booking platform fits your business, we will configure that rather than build something — it is faster and cheaper, and we would rather tell you that than sell you a build you do not need. Custom makes sense when your pricing, scheduling rules or workflow are genuinely unusual, when you need it joined to systems you already run, or when per-user subscription fees stop making sense at your size. We work this out on the first call and give you both options with real numbers.

Who this is for

Clinics and dental practices, salons and barbershops, physical therapy and wellness studios, tutoring centers, repair and home-service businesses, consultants, and anyone else whose revenue depends on a full calendar. If you're currently taking bookings through text messages and a paper diary, this is usually the single highest-return change you can make.

Frequently asked questions

Can customers book without calling us?

Yes — that is the point. They see your real availability and confirm a slot themselves, any time of day. You can still take phone bookings and enter them manually; both land in the same calendar.

Will it work with the calendar I already use?

Usually yes. Google Calendar and Outlook sync both ways, so a slot blocked in your personal calendar disappears from the booking page automatically.

How do reminders reduce no-shows?

People forget, and a reminder with a one-tap reschedule option gives them an easy alternative to simply not turning up. Combined with a small deposit at booking, most businesses see a clear drop. The exact effect depends on your customer base, so we would rather you measure it than take a number from us.

Do we need technical staff to run it?

No. The daily view is a calendar and a customer list. We train your team on a short call and leave a written guide. Anything genuinely technical stays our job.

Can it handle multiple staff members and locations?

Yes. Each staff member can have their own services, hours and calendar, and each location its own schedule, all behind one booking page.

Can we take payments or deposits at booking?

Yes. Deposits and prepayment run through Stripe or Square, built into the booking flow rather than a separate step. Taking even a small deposit is the most reliable way to cut no-shows.

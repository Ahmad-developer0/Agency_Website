Web Development in Faisalabad

Nexyra Studio was founded in Faisalabad in 2022 and still works from here. Most of our clients are remote — UK, US, UAE, Australia — but working with a local business is different in ways that matter, and this page is about that difference.

What being local actually changes

  • We can meet. For a first conversation about a business you have run for twenty years, sitting across a table beats a video call. We come to you.
  • We work in the language you prefer. Urdu, Punjabi or English — whichever makes it easier to explain what you need. The website itself can be in English, Urdu, or both.
  • Payment is straightforward. Local bank transfer, no international invoicing or currency conversion.
  • Same working week. You message during your working day and get a reply during ours, with no overnight gap.

Local businesses we build for

Faisalabad's business mix is its own thing, and a site that fits a London consultancy often does not fit here.

  • Textile and manufacturing — the city's core trade. Usually a credibility site: what you produce, capacity, certifications, and a clear enquiry route for buyers who found you at an expo or through a referral. Often the audience is overseas, so the site has to work for a buyer who has never visited.
  • Clinics, dental practices and salons — where online booking and reminders replace a register and a phone that nobody answers after hours.
  • Retail, wholesale and distribution — catalogue and ordering, or a full store where it earns its keep.
  • Education and training centres — courses, admissions enquiries and fee information.
  • Property and construction — listings, project galleries and enquiry capture.

Getting found in Faisalabad

For a business serving customers in this city, your Google Business Profile matters more than your website's search ranking. It is what shows in Google Maps and in the local results that sit above everything else, and it is free. Most local businesses have one that is half filled in, or none at all.

We set it up properly — categories, services, hours, service area, photos — make sure your name, address and phone number match everywhere they appear, and put a review flow in place so satisfied customers are actually asked. Combined with a site that loads fast on a phone, that is most of what local search needs. The full picture is on our SEO and digital marketing page.

Built for how people here browse

Almost all of your visitors will arrive on a phone, often on mobile data rather than wi-fi, sometimes on an older Android device. That shapes how we build: mobile layout first, compressed images, minimal JavaScript, and pages that are usable before everything has finished loading. A heavy site that looks impressive on a designer's laptop and takes eight seconds on a phone is a site that loses customers.

We also put the contact route where people expect it. In Pakistan that usually means a WhatsApp button, not only an email form — most enquiries arrive that way, and a site that makes people hunt for it loses them.

How to start

A free 30-minute conversation, in person here in Faisalabad or on a call, whichever suits you. Tell us what you sell and what currently gets in the way. You get an honest view of what is worth building, roughly what it costs and how long it takes. If the right answer is a simpler or cheaper option than the one you came in asking for, we will say that.

Frequently asked questions

Can we meet in person in Faisalabad?

Yes. For local clients we are happy to meet for the first conversation and for major reviews. Day-to-day work then runs on WhatsApp and email, which is faster for both sides than scheduling meetings.

Do you only work with Faisalabad businesses?

No, most of our work is remote with clients in the UK, US, UAE and Australia. This page is about what changes when a client is local, not a restriction on who we work with.

Can the website be in Urdu?

Yes — Urdu, English, or both with a language switch. Which makes sense depends on who buys from you. An exporter's site usually needs English; a local service business often does better with both.

How do payments work for local clients?

Local bank transfer, in stages tied to agreed milestones, with a written scope and fixed price before any work starts.

Will you help with Google Maps listing?

Yes, and for a business serving customers in Faisalabad it is usually the highest-return thing we can do. It is free to set up and often brings enquiries before the website ranks for anything.

Do you work with textile and export businesses?

Yes. Those sites have a different job — proving capability to a buyer who may never visit, with clear production details, certifications and a straightforward enquiry route.
